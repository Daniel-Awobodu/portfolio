## How the 20 were chosen

1. I ran 14 YouTube searches ("FPL tips", "FPL strategy", "Fantasy Premier League tips", "FPL team selection", "FPL best team", "FPL wildcard", "FPL chip strategy", "FPL draft 2026/27", "how to win at FPL", "FPL transfers", "FPL captain", "FPL beginner guide", "FPL template", "FPL differentials"), each filtered to this year and sorted by views, taking the top 50 results per search.
2. I kept videos whose title mentions FPL or Fantasy Premier League, lasting 3 minutes to 2 hours, uploaded on or after 2026-04-17, and not livestreams or deadline streams. Livestreams are hours of chat Q&A, not structured advice.
3. I ranked them by view count, capped at 3 per channel. Without the cap, two channels would hold about 40 of the top 45 slots, and repetition across videos would just be one person repeating themselves.
4. I downloaded English captions: creator-uploaded where available, otherwise YouTube's auto-generated track.

## Exclusions and corrections

- **Box2BoxShow, "DRAFTING OUR PREMIER LEAGUE FANTASY SQUADS! (FPL)"** (283k views, would have ranked #2): this is an *FPL Draft* league (snake draft, waivers, trades, no budget), a different game from classic FPL, so it is excluded.
- **FPL Harry, "FPL GW1 DRAFTS AND DISCUSSION WITH @FPLRaptor & @FPLFocal"** (69k): YouTube listed this collab as "FPL Harry and 2 more", so the first run counted it outside Harry's cap. Counted correctly, it would be Harry's 4th video, so it is excluded.
- **FPL Harry, "MY FPL GW19 TRANSFER PLANS"** (362k): a 2025/26 video, older than the window.
- **Two runs:** the second run (after the fixes above) hit temporary YouTube throttling on #2, #3 and #4: missing captions on two, a connection reset on the third. The selection above is the true view ranking, with those three taken from the first run's identical transcripts. #19 and #20 come from the second run. `scripts/fetch_fpl_transcripts.py` now retries those temporary errors.

## Known limitations

- **Early season only.** The sample is almost all 2026/27 preseason to GW6. There is little on double and blank gameweeks or second-half chips.
- **Video type.** View-ranking favours gameweek team-reveal and wildcard videos. Pure strategy guides ("FPL CHIPS EXPLAINED", "10 Tips for FPL Top 10K", "Complete FPL 2026/27 CHIP Guide") were just outside the top 20 under the channel cap.
- **Caption errors.** Auto-captions misspell player names (e.g. "Mehamerovic" / "Muharemovic", "Califury" for Calafiori). Check names against the official game.
- **Transcripts are not committed** to this public repo, because they are the creators' content. Re-create them locally with the script; they go to the git-ignored `transcripts/` folder.
