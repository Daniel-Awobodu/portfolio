## Also worth knowing (made by only 1-2 videos)

- Activate chips and save your team well before the deadline. An elite manager missed it, lost his wildcard and took a -64 (#11).
- Check the *result* of a midweek European first leg: a comfortable lead means rotation in the second leg, and possibly at the weekend (#19).
- Pick your £4.0m bench keeper from a team you won't triple up on, and one unlikely to drop in price (#5).
- Keep a written list of every candidate per position, so a wildcard doesn't forget anyone (#8).
- Separate your "watching brain" (weekend, emotional) from your "decision-making mode" (later in the week, detached) (#17).
- A forward who gets lots of big chances but finishes poorly vs one who converts few chances well is a genuine trade-off: volume is more repeatable (#8, #12).
- The managers' own track records (5x top 10k, former #1, #1 all-time average) don't stop them admitting regular mistakes. Most of their "lessons" come from regret about over-reacting, over-planning, or fading the best defence (#2, #3, #5, #8).
