## The problem

quality-buy.online is my own store, and I run it partly as a laboratory. The
problem it solves for clients is simple: most people selling online cannot tell
you which part of their funnel is broken. They know sales are low. They do not
know whether it is the audience, the creative, the offer, or the page.

I wanted a place where I could break each part on purpose and watch what
happened, without spending someone else's budget to find out.

## What I built

A deliberately boring account structure, so results are readable.

One campaign per objective. Broad audiences rather than a stack of narrow
interest sets, because narrow sets stop being meaningful once the pixel has
data. Creative tested in threes — same offer, three different opening hooks —
so I am always testing one variable, not four.

Every result gets written into a **Google Sheet** by hand, weekly. Manual on
purpose: writing the numbers down is what makes you notice the pattern.

## How it works, explained simply

Selling with ads is really four questions, asked in order.

Did the right person see it? If nobody relevant saw the ad, nothing else
matters. Did the ad make them stop? That is the hook — the first line and the
first second. Did the page make them want it? That is the offer and the sales
page doing their job. Did checkout make it easy to say yes?

A funnel only ever breaks at one of those four points. The whole skill is
knowing which one you are looking at, and changing only that.
