## The problem

[CLIENT NAME] was paying for traffic that arrived and left. The ads were doing
their job — click-through was healthy — but the page converted at [0.0%]. The
instinct in the room was to change the ads. The ads were not the problem.

## What I built

I started by reading, not writing. Every WhatsApp conversation, every comment,
every abandoned-checkout reply from the previous [3 months]. One question came
up again and again, in slightly different words: *what happens if it does not
work for me?*

The old page never answered it. So I rebuilt the page around that answer —
moved the guarantee above the fold, put real customer messages next to it,
replaced three paragraphs of product description with one photo sequence showing
the product in use, and cut the form from [7] fields to [3].

## How it works, explained simply

A sales page is one side of a conversation, and the visitor is silently asking
questions the whole way down.

If you answer them in the order they are asked, they keep reading. If you answer
a question they have not asked yet — the specification before the "will this
work for me" — they stop, because you sound like you are talking about yourself.

So you do not write a sales page. You write down the questions in order, then
answer them one at a time. The page is just the transcript.

## The outcome

Conversion rate moved from [0.0%] to [0.0%] on the same ad spend and the same
audiences, which took cost per purchase down [0%]. Nothing about the traffic
changed — only the order the page answered questions in.
