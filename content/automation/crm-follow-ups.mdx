## The problem

At a real estate brokerage, agents forgot to follow up with warm leads, and
when they did, the messages felt copy-pasted. Leads went cold on both counts:
there was no reliable rhythm, and no real personal touch when contact did
happen.

## What I built

Three connected workflows in n8n, sharing one Google Sheet as the record of
where every lead stands.

![The three n8n workflows: new lead, reply watcher and scheduled follow-up](/case-studies/crm-follow-ups/workflow.png)

*The three lanes. Top: a new lead gets its first message. Middle: replies are
watched. Bottom: follow-ups go out on schedule.*

1. **A new lead gets a personal first email.** When a contact enters HubSpot as
   a new lead, AI writes a first message that mentions their preferred area,
   budget and what they last asked about. It goes out through Gmail.
2. **Silence gets two follow-ups.** If the lead does not reply, a follow-up
   goes out on day 3 and another on day 7. Each one continues the same email
   thread instead of arriving as a new, disconnected email.
3. **A reply stops everything.** The moment a genuine reply lands, the
   sequence ends, the lead moves to "Responded" in HubSpot, and the assigned
   agent gets a follow-up task.

## How it works, explained simply

It is a sales assistant with a perfect memory, who writes like a person and
steps back the moment the client answers.

- **It checks again right before every send.** A follow-up is not sent just
  because it was scheduled. The lead's status is rechecked seconds before
  sending, so a reply that lands just before a follow-up still stops it. That
  exact situation came up during testing, and the follow-up was correctly
  held back.
- **It matches replies to the right conversation.** Replies are matched by the
  email thread, not by email address, because aliases and forwarded replies
  can make addresses unreliable.
- **It never mistakes its own emails for replies.** During testing, the
  system's own sent emails were briefly being read as replies. Filtering by
  Gmail's sent label fixed it for good.
- **Late replies still count.** If a lead replies after the sequence has
  already ended, the reply is still caught and handed to an agent.
- **Missing details are handled gracefully.** If a lead's area or budget is
  unknown, the AI simply leaves it out instead of writing an awkward blank.

## The outcome

The whole lead journey runs on its own: a personal first message, timed
follow-ups, reply detection and a clean handoff to a human, with no one
watching an inbox.
