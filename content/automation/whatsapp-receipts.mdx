## The problem

A delivery company's drivers already sent photos of their fuel and repair
receipts over WhatsApp. But someone in the office still had to open each photo
and type the vendor, date and amount into a spreadsheet by hand. It was slow,
typing errors crept in, and receipts got lost whenever nobody followed up in
time.

## What I built

An n8n workflow that turns a WhatsApp message into a filed, logged expense.

![The receipt workflow in n8n, from the incoming WhatsApp message to the Drive upload, the Sheets row and the confirmation](/case-studies/whatsapp-receipts/workflow.png)

*The full workflow. The top path handles a clear receipt from start to finish.
The lower branches catch bad files, unclear readings and save failures.*

1. **A driver sends a receipt** to a WhatsApp number, as a photo or a PDF.
   Anything else gets a polite reply asking for the right format.
2. **AI reads it.** Google Gemini extracts the vendor, date, total, tax and
   category. Photos and PDFs take separate paths, then join up again.
3. **The driver is matched** by phone number against a roster in Airtable.
4. **The reading is checked.** Clear readings move on. Unclear ones are logged
   for review, and the driver is asked to resend.
5. **The receipt is filed.** It goes into a Google Drive folder for the
   current month, named by date, vendor and driver, and a row is added to the
   expense sheet.
6. **The driver gets a WhatsApp confirmation** of exactly what was logged.

## How it works, explained simply

It behaves like an office assistant who reads every receipt the moment it
arrives, and says so when a number is hard to read instead of guessing.

- **A wrong number is worse than a missing one.** A misread total looks just
  like a correct one once it sits in a spreadsheet. So the AI flags any field
  it was not sure about, and those receipts go to a review log instead of
  quietly becoming "data".
- **Unknown drivers are still logged.** If a number is not in the roster, the
  receipt is saved under "Unknown Driver" rather than dropped.
- **No messy folders.** The workflow checks for this month's Drive folder
  before creating one, so receipts never scatter across duplicate folders.
- **Nothing fails silently.** A failed download, a wrong file type or a failed
  save each sends a clear message to the driver, and to the admin when it
  matters.

## The outcome

A receipt goes from a driver's WhatsApp to a filed, logged expense in about a
minute, and nobody in the office touches a spreadsheet.

<NextStep>

**A reminder for unclear receipts.** A receipt flagged as unclear waits in the
review log until the driver resends it. An automatic nudge to the driver would
close that loop.

**A fallback when WhatsApp fails.** If WhatsApp can't deliver a reply, nothing
else steps in yet. A second channel, such as SMS, would make sure the driver
always hears back.
