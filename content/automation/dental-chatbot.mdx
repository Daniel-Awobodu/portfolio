## The problem

This is an industry build: a complete, working system built to a realistic
brief from a dental practice.

The practice's website chat brought in a steady stream of enquiries, but a
person had to qualify and book every single one. Replies were slow, interested
patients went cold, and the calendar kept gaps that could have been filled.

## What I built

An AI chat assistant in n8n that talks to visitors, works out what they need,
and acts on it.

![A website chat: the assistant welcomes the visitor, who asks about teeth whitening, and the assistant asks if they are a new or existing patient](/case-studies/dental-chatbot/chat.png)

*The chat in action. The assistant answers naturally, then asks the next
qualifying question.*

1. **It qualifies in conversation, not with a form.** The assistant asks
   whether the visitor is a new or existing patient, which service they want,
   and how urgent it is.
2. **It decides the next step.** Every visitor goes down one of three routes:
   book an appointment, join a nurture list for follow-up, or, for urgent
   symptoms, get an emergency safety message straight away.
3. **It books real slots.** For bookings, it checks the live Google Calendar,
   offers open times, and creates the appointment once the visitor picks one.
4. **It keeps a record.** Nurture leads, emergencies and errors are logged to
   a Google Sheet so the team can follow up.

![The n8n workflow behind the chat assistant: the AI step, a router, and branches for booking, nurture, emergency and fallback replies](/case-studies/dental-chatbot/workflow.png)

*The workflow. One AI step reads the conversation, a router picks the route,
and each branch handles booking, nurture or an emergency. Click the image to

## How it works, explained simply

Think of it as a front desk receptionist who never sleeps, never forgets a
detail, and always checks the diary before promising a time.

- **Safety comes first.** Emergency symptoms are checked before anything else,
  so an urgent case never gets stuck in a booking conversation.
- **The AI never invents a time.** The assistant only hands off the request.
  The real open slots always come from the calendar, so the one fact that
  matters most never comes from the AI.
- **A slot is rechecked the moment it is picked.** Two visitors, or a staff
  member, could grab the same time mid-conversation. Rechecking the calendar
  right before booking caught exactly that during testing, and it is closed.
- **No double bookings from one chat.** Once a visit is confirmed, the same
  conversation cannot accidentally book a second one.
- **Bad AI output does not crash it.** If the AI replies in a broken format,
  it is caught and flagged instead of failing silently.

## The outcome

One chat handles emergency triage, live booking and nurture follow-up, with
no staff involved from the first "hi" to a booked appointment.

One known gap, stated plainly: visitors who go quiet halfway through a chat
are not yet added to the nurture list automatically. That is the next thing I
would add for a practice running this live.
