## The problem

At a SaaS company, the operations team ran the whole client journey by hand.
Each new lead took up to 20 minutes to log, add to the CRM and follow up, so
hot leads waited hours for a reply. Every Stripe payment had to be copied into
the CRM and a spreadsheet. And every Monday, the ops manager spent 45 minutes
pulling numbers from three tools to build a report.

Three manual jobs, and all of them depended on someone being free to do them.

## What I built

Three connected workflows in n8n that cover the journey from first contact,
to payment, to the weekly report.

![The three n8n workflows on one canvas: Stripe payment sync, the weekly report, and lead intake](/case-studies/client-lifecycle/workflow.png)

*All three workflows. Top: payment sync. Middle: weekly report. Bottom: lead
intake.*

1. **Lead intake.** The moment someone fills in the website form, the lead is
   checked, added to HubSpot, sent a welcome SMS through Termii, announced to
   the sales team in Slack, and logged in Google Sheets.
2. **Payment sync.** Every completed Stripe payment finds the customer in
   HubSpot, updates their plan and last payment date, and is logged to a
   payment sheet.
3. **Weekly report.** Every Monday at 8:30am, the workflow reads the payment
   log, totals the last 7 days by revenue, number of payments and plan, and
   sends the report by email and Slack.

## How it works, explained simply

Together, the three workflows act as an operations assistant who answers every
new lead in seconds, never misses a payment, and has the Monday numbers ready
before the meeting starts.

- **The workflows feed each other.** The weekly report only works because the
  payment sync logs clean data every single time. That is the difference
  between a few separate automations and one system.
- **Incomplete leads don't break anything.** A form that arrives without a
  name, email or phone number goes to a Failed Leads sheet instead of
  crashing the workflow, so nothing is lost.
- **Phone numbers are cleaned first.** Spaces and stray characters are
  removed, and the number is put into international format before the SMS
  goes out.
- **It records whether each SMS went out.** The workflow reads Termii's
  response and marks each lead's row Yes or No, instead of assuming.
- **No payment goes untracked.** If a paying customer is not in HubSpot, or
  the payment log fails to save, the team gets an email with the payment
  details so someone can act on it.
- **It tries again before giving up.** If HubSpot is briefly unavailable, the
  step retries up to 3 times before anything is flagged.

## The outcome

A new lead now gets a reply in under 3 seconds, every payment lands in the CRM
and the log, and the Monday report arrives on its own. Nobody on the team has
to copy, paste or remember any of it.
