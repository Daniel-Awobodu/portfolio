## The problem

A solar installation company in the United States runs ads that send
homeowners to a form on its website. After every submission, someone had to
research the lead, score it, update the CRM and write the first email by hand.
Leads waited, and the sales team spent their time on admin instead of calls.

## What I built

A workflow in n8n that takes a form submission all the way to a scored lead
in HubSpot and a first contact, with no one touching it.

![The n8n workflow: the form trigger, validation, the returning-lead check, address verification, four enrichment steps, AI scoring, the HubSpot update, then email for HOT and WARM leads or a WhatsApp alert for leads that need review](/case-studies/solar-leads/workflow.png)

*The workflow, from form to first contact. The branches below the main line
handle renters, returning leads and addresses that can't be verified.*

1. **It checks the form.** Leads with missing details, no consent to be
   contacted or a broken email address are logged as disqualified. So are
   renters, because only an owner can approve an installation.
2. **It spots returning leads.** Someone filling in the form again is already
   interested, so the sales team gets a WhatsApp message to call them
   instead of an automated email.
3. **It verifies the address.** The address must match a real US building
   with at least 90% confidence. If it doesn't, the lead is logged and sales
   are asked to confirm it by phone. Nothing is researched on a guessed
   address.
4. **It researches the property:** the property record (owner on the deed,
   size, roof type, year built), the local sunlight from NREL, and the
   state's electricity price from the EIA, which is saved for a month so the
   same state isn't looked up twice.
5. **AI does the lead scoring.** Each lead gets a score from 0 to 100 and a
   tier: HOT, WARM or REVIEW. Every score comes with its reasons, concerns and
   a recommended next step.
6. **Everything goes into HubSpot**, then each tier is followed up. HOT and
   WARM leads get a personal first email. REVIEW leads go to a person on
   WhatsApp, with the reasons.

## How it works, explained simply

Every lead is researched before anyone calls, what the homeowner says is
checked against public records, and the system knows which calls need a
person.

- **Every fact is labelled.** Each detail is tagged with where it came from
  (the homeowner, a public record, or an estimate) and how confident the
  system is in it. The AI is told to treat an estimate as an estimate.
- **What the lead says is checked against the records.** If the name isn't
  on the deed, or the roof type on the form doesn't match the county record,
  the lead goes to REVIEW, whatever its score. A good score can't hide a red
  flag.
- **The first email only uses facts it can trust.** It may mention the bill
  range, roof, timeline, home size and sunlight, and only when they are
  high confidence. It never mentions savings, system size or roof direction,
  because those were estimated, not measured.
- **The tone matches the lead.** A HOT lead is nudged toward a quick call. A
  WARM lead gets a low-pressure note that only asks for a reply.
- **If the AI fails, the lead isn't lost.** An unreadable score sends the
  lead to REVIEW for a person to handle.
- **Outages don't stop it.** If a property or sunlight lookup fails, the lead
  is still scored with what is known. If the address check fails, the run
  stops and a separate workflow sends the error to WhatsApp.

## The outcome

Every lead is researched, scored and logged within minutes of submitting the
form, and the sales team only steps in where a person is actually needed.

![Two first-touch emails: a HOT lead asked for a 15-minute call, and a WARM lead offered a short summary with no pressure](/case-studies/solar-leads/first-emails.png)

*Top: a HOT lead, ready to move, asked for a quick call. Bottom: a WARM lead,
still researching, offered a short summary instead.*

Each path was tested: a HOT lead and a WARM lead were emailed, a returning
lead and an unverifiable address went to sales on WhatsApp, a renter was
disqualified, a lead with contradictions went to review, and a forced error
reached WhatsApp with the failed step named.
