## The problem

[CLIENT NAME] was getting enquiries from four places at once — Facebook lead
forms, WhatsApp, the website contact form, and Instagram DMs — and all four
landed in different places. Nobody owned the inbox, so the average enquiry sat
for about [12 hours] before a human saw it. Serious buyers had already booked a
viewing with someone else by then.

## What I built

A single intake pipeline. Every enquiry, whatever channel it arrives on, gets
pushed into one **n8n** workflow. That workflow cleans the data, writes the lead
into a **Supabase** table, and scores it against a simple set of rules the team
agreed on — budget band, location, and whether the person named a timeline.

High-scoring leads trigger an instant WhatsApp acknowledgement through the
**WhatsApp Cloud API** and a notification to the agent on duty. Everything else
goes into a nurture sequence. A daily digest lands in the sales lead's inbox at
8am with what came in and what still needs a human.

## How it works, explained simply

Think of it as a receptionist who never sleeps.

Every message that arrives gets handed to the same person at the front desk, no
matter which door it came through. That receptionist writes the visitor's
details in one book, so nothing is lost. Then they ask two or three quick
questions to work out how ready the visitor is to buy. Ready buyers get walked
straight to an agent; everyone else gets a polite note and a follow-up later.

The only difference is that this receptionist answers in four minutes, at 2am,
on a public holiday.

## The outcome

Response time dropped from about [12 hours] to under [4 minutes], and the team
stopped spending [roughly a morning a week] copying leads between tabs. The
sales lead can now see, in one table, where every enquiry came from and what
happened to it — which is the part they say changed how they plan their week.
