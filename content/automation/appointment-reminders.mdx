## The problem

A clinic's old reminder automation was fragile. It texted patients about
appointments that had already been moved or cancelled, and sometimes at the
wrong time. Patients stopped trusting the messages.

The root cause was simple: each reminder was scheduled once and never checked
again. If staff moved an appointment after that, the old reminder still went
out.

## What I built

A scheduled workflow in n8n that treats every reminder as unconfirmed until
the moment it is about to send.

![The reminder workflow in n8n: fetch upcoming appointments, filter out reminders already sent, wait, recheck the live calendar, then send the SMS or log the skip](/case-studies/appointment-reminders/workflow.png)

*The full workflow. The recheck happens right before sending, and every
outcome is logged, whether the reminder was sent or skipped.*

1. **Every 15 minutes**, it fetches the appointments coming up in the next 25
   hours from Google Calendar.
2. **It works out which reminders are due:** one 24 hours before each visit
   and one 2 hours before.
3. **It skips anything already sent**, using a log of every reminder.
4. **It waits briefly, then rechecks the live calendar** for that exact
   appointment, so last-minute staff changes land first.
5. **If the appointment moved or was cancelled**, the old reminder is skipped
   and the reason is logged.
6. **If it is still valid**, the phone number is put into the right format
   and the SMS goes out through Termii. The result is checked and logged.

## How it works, explained simply

The old system trusted a note written yesterday. This one looks at the diary
again right before picking up the phone.

- **A reminder is never trusted until it sends.** The appointment is fetched
  fresh from the calendar seconds before each text goes out, not just when
  the reminder was first planned.
- **A moved appointment counts as a new one.** The sent log records each
  appointment together with its exact start time. Change the time, and the
  system treats it as a fresh booking, so it can never reuse an old,
  wrong reminder.
- **Nothing is skipped silently.** Every cancelled or moved appointment is
  logged with the reason it was skipped.
- **It checks the send result.** The workflow reads Termii's response for
  every text instead of assuming it went through.

## The outcome

Reminders now match the calendar at the moment they are sent, not the moment
they were planned. Nine test cases passed with zero stale reminders. In one
live test, a queued appointment was dragged to a new time mid-flow, and the
system skipped the old reminder and logged exactly why.
